# Scented Flowers Museum Operations

## Public rule
Annual honoree identities are never published before the visit. Public pages sell the museum world, annual change, city, dates, venue, and ticket access — not the roster.

## Internal command center
`/museum-boh` is a noindex internal route. Authenticated active organization members can access:
- Lead Command: museum, city, intent, status, priority, next action.
- Curatorial: private annual honorees, source count, ten-step verification/rights queue.
- Tour + Tickets: planned → announced → on_sale lifecycle, venue, dates, and ticket URL.

## Ticket activation
Updating a tour city to `announced` creates announced ticket offers. Updating it to `on_sale` requires a ticket URL and activates General Admission. Public museum pages read only announced/on-sale inventory. Honoree identities remain private throughout.

## Curatorial guardrail
The supplied museum source decks are recorded as internal references confirming annual collection inclusion and museum framing only. They are not treated as sufficient evidence for biographies, awards, quotes, family relationships, artifact provenance, or media rights. Those fields require separate verified sources and rights review.
