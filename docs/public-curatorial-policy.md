# Public Curatorial Policy

The complete annual honoree lineup for Living Legends, Fallen Stars, and Women Make the World Go Round is an internal curatorial asset until the museum experience reveals it.

## Public messaging
- The collection changes every year.
- Each edition features different stories and experiences.
- The complete lineup is discovered inside the museum.

## Do not publish
- Full annual honoree rosters on public pages.
- Public person-by-person digital exhibit directories before reveal.
- Search-indexable honoree profile routes tied to the current annual collection.

## Internal systems
Curatorial records may remain in Supabase and internal workflows, protected from anonymous/public read access.
